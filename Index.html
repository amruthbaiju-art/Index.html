ENVIRONMENTAL STUDIES TERM PROJECT

Plastic Waste Management

Understanding the problems caused by plastic waste and exploring practical ways to reduce, reuse and manage plastic responsibly.

1. Project Overview

Plastic is useful, lightweight and inexpensive, but its long-lasting nature has created serious waste-management problems. This project looks at common plastic types, their environmental effects, recycling and practical solutions that can be applied on a college campus.

400+ Million Tons

More than 400 million tonnes of plastic are produced globally each year.

Low Recycling

Only a relatively small share of plastic waste is successfully recycled.

Long-lasting Waste

Many plastics can remain in the environment for decades or longer.

3. Environmental Impact

Microplastics

Larger plastic items can break down into tiny particles that can enter soil and water systems.

Marine Pollution

Plastic waste can reach rivers and oceans, where it can harm marine organisms through ingestion and entanglement.

Open Burning

Burning plastic in uncontrolled conditions can release harmful pollutants into the air.

4. Solutions & Circular Economy

Plastic waste management works best when we reduce waste at the source and keep useful materials in circulation instead of simply following a “take, make and throw away” model.

Reduce & Reuse

Avoid unnecessary single-use plastic and choose reusable bottles, bags, cups and containers.

Recycling

Separate clean recyclable plastics and send them through appropriate collection and recycling systems.

Better Policies

Producer responsibility, proper collection systems and awareness programmes can improve plastic management.

5. Campus Action Plan

A simple four-step plan that can be followed within a college campus:

  • Segregate waste: Keep wet waste and dry recyclable waste in separate bins.
  • Reduce single-use plastic: Encourage reusable bottles, cups and cutlery in canteens.
  • Collection drives: Conduct regular student-led plastic collection and recycling drives.
  • Spread awareness: Use posters, workshops and digital displays to encourage responsible plastic use.
